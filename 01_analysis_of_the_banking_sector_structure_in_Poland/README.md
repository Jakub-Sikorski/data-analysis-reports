# Analysis of the Polish Banking Sector Structure (2009-2025)

## Project Description
This project presents an analysis of structural changes in the Polish banking sector over the last dozen or so years. The study is based on official statistical reports from the Polish Financial Supervision Authority. To ensure measurement consistency, only data from the end of each billing period was extracted, excluding the incomplete year 2026. The goal of the analysis is to identify long-term trends related to digitalization, market consolidation, and cost optimization of financial institutions.

## Technologies Used
* **Python**
* **Pandas:** Cleaning, aggregating, and transforming KNF data.
* **Matplotlib:** Creating clear visualizations of trends and percentage structure.

## Key Findings
* **Market consolidation:** For over a decade, there has been a clear downward trend in the number of commercial and cooperative banks, resulting from mergers and acquisitions forced by increasing capital requirements.
* **Fragmentation of the cooperative sector:** Despite the overall decline in the number of entities, cooperative banks consistently account for about 90% of all institutions in the Polish market.
* **Impact of digitalization on infrastructure:** After the peak period of 2011–2013, there was a drastic, mass closure of domestic branches, which is a direct consequence of clients shifting to online and mobile banking.
* **Local nature of the sector:** Domestic branches constitute over 99% of the entire physical infrastructure. The foreign network of Polish banks is marginal and shows virtually no expansion.
